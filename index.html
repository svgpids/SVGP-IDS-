CAMPUS FACILITIES

Facilities for a Better Learning Experience

Well-equipped infrastructure and student support for a brighter future.

👥
Staff

The highly motivated teaching and non-teaching staff strive a lot in imparting quality technical education to the students.

>
🧪
Laboratories

Each branch has several well-established laboratories and also well-equipped workshop where students gain practical knowledge.

>
⚙️
AP Skill Development Centre

The Centre imparts training to the students in Proge Cad, Solid Edge, Total Station, VM software which enable students to become employable.

>
🛡️
National Cadet Corps (NCC)

The NCC unit functions with an intake of 100 boy and girl cadets. It provides an opportunity for students to get B and C certificates.

>
🤝
National Service Scheme (NSS)

The NSS unit with an intake of 100 volunteers provides an opportunity to the students to participate in community development.

>
💼
Campus Placements

Several reputed companies conduct placement drives in the campus wherein students are placed in top industries.

>
🏠
Hostel

Well established hostel facilities for boys and girls with clean dining and security.

>
🏆
Sports and Games

Students practice in the sprawling playground and excel in inter-polytechnic sports meets.

>
📖
Library

Well stocked central library on diverse technical titles and journals for student knowledge.
